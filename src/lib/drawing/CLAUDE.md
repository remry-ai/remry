# Drawings — `src/lib/drawing/`

A drawing is a wiki page or a doc with `bodyType: drawing`, and only that: it's never shown inside other text. Claude writes a **skeleton** (shapes, labels and arrows, no coordinates; `$shared/utils/drawing`, pure: `parseDrawingContent`, `layoutSkeleton`, `colorSkeleton`); the skill's `references/drawings.md` has the format. The server side (storing, search, share links, Pro limit) is in `src/api/CLAUDE.md` § Wiki pages.

- **Shown as a picture:** `DrawingImage`, a PNG from `render-drawing.ts` (Excalidraw's canvas export, which uses the page's fonts, so the CSP needs nothing more). Clicking it, or the pencil, opens `DrawingEditor` over the whole window; in a peek it opens in `ZoomViewer`.
- **The editor** loads Excalidraw (MIT, bundled with React) only when it opens, mounted with `createRoot` (no JSX) on `<body>`. A skeleton is laid out and expanded with Excalidraw's `convertToExcalidrawElements` (`excalidraw.ts`, `toScene`), in the default branding's colours where Claude set none; the first edit saves it as Excalidraw's own scene. It saves after a short pause, and the × waits for the save (Esc stays with Excalidraw).
- **Offline:** Excalidraw's fonts are served by the app at `/excalidraw/fonts/` (`scripts/vite/excalidraw-fonts.ts`, dev middleware and copied into the build; Xiaolai, the 13 MB CJK font, left out), and `keepFontsLocal` (`font-sources.ts`) strips Excalidraw's CDN fallbacks. The editor hides everything that reaches outside: the library browser, Excalidraw+ and social links (the menu is our own), Excalidraw's export-to-link, web embeds (the CSP refuses iframes) and the image tool (so no base64 lands in the database).
- **Files and links:** the menu opens and saves `.excalidraw` files, and `page.create`/`page.update` take one's JSON as content (pictures left out). **Share link** and **From an Excalidraw link** are the one way a drawing leaves the computer: `drawing.shareLink` uploads a copy encrypted here to `json.excalidraw.com`, and `drawing.importLink` fetches and decrypts one, both on the server, only when the user asks, through `src/shared/drawing/excalidraw-backend.server.ts` (that one host, a timeout, a 5 MB cap). The key exists only in the link. Don't widen it to other hosts or to anything not asked for.
- **No embeds:** an old fenced `drawing` block in markdown shows as a "Drawing ↗" link to its page (`drawing-links.ts`), with no fetch and no Excalidraw.
